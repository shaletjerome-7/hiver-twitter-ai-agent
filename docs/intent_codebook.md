# Intent Codebook

## billing

Charges, invoices, subscriptions, payment failures.

Examples

- charged twice
- billing issue
- premium payment

---

## playback

Music cannot play, buffering, offline mode, advertisements.

Examples

- songs skip
- music won't play
- buffering

---

## login

Authentication issues.

Examples

- cannot login
- password reset
- account locked

---

## account

Security or account ownership issues.

Examples

- hacked account
- suspicious login
- email changed

Always escalate.

---

## refund

Refund requests.

Examples

- refund
- money back
- accidental purchase

Always escalate.

---

## other

Anything outside the supported taxonomy.
